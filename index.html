Tovie Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Tovie is a personal movie, TV show, and anime tracking app. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Data we do not collect

Tovie has no user accounts and no login, and does not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, or any other directly identifying personal information. Tovie shows no advertising and does not use any data for advertising. The limited, anonymous usage and diagnostic data described under "Third-party services we use" below is collected through Google's Firebase services.

Data stored on your device

Everything you track in Tovie — the movies, TV shows, and anime you add, your watch status, episode progress, ratings, and favorites — is stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted anywhere, except when you deliberately choose to export a backup file yourself (see below).

Third-party services we use

The Movie Database (TMDB): Tovie uses TMDB's API to retrieve movie, TV show, and anime information (titles, artwork, cast, ratings, and similar metadata). When you search or browse, your search terms and requests are sent to TMDB to retrieve this information. Tovie does not send TMDB any personal information about you. See TMDB's own privacy policy for how they handle this. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google): If Tovie crashes or encounters an error, technical diagnostic information — such as your device model, Android version, app version, an anonymous installation identifier, and a stack trace describing what went wrong — is sent to Google's Crashlytics service so we can identify and fix the issue. This diagnostic data is not linked to your tracked shows, watch history, or any personal information. See Google's Firebase privacy information for details.

Google Analytics for Firebase (Google): Tovie uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used and to measure interest in upcoming features. Through this service, Google receives an anonymous app-instance identifier, your device's Android advertising ID (where your device provides one), approximate location derived from your masked IP address, basic device and app information, and usage events such as app sessions and screen views. Tovie also records a single custom event if you tap "I'm interested" on the Tovie Premium screen; this records only that the tap happened, not who you are. This data is used only for analytics: it is not used for advertising, is not linked to your name or any account (Tovie has none), and does not include your tracked titles, watch history, or search terms. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Firebase Remote Config (Google): Tovie periodically downloads a small configuration value from Google's Firebase servers. It currently controls whether Tovie Premium features are free for everyone during the evaluation period. To deliver it, your device sends Google its country code, language, time zone, Android and app version, and an anonymous Firebase installation identifier. No payment information is involved: Tovie does not currently sell or charge for anything.

Your backups

Tovie lets you export a backup of your local data to a file. This export is only ever initiated by you, and the resulting file is saved wherever you choose (your device storage, a cloud drive, etc., at your discretion). Tovie itself does not upload or transmit this file anywhere. If you choose to set up automatic backup (a Tovie Premium feature, currently free), Tovie periodically saves a copy of your data to a folder on your device that you select; it still never leaves your device unless that folder is synced by an app or service you chose yourself.

Data retention and your choices

Because the analytics and diagnostic data described above is not linked to your name or to any account, we cannot look up or delete an individual person's records on request. It is retained according to Google's Firebase data-retention settings. Uninstalling Tovie stops all further collection from your device, and you can limit advertising-ID access in your Android settings. Questions can be sent to the contact address below.

Children's privacy

Tovie is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If Tovie's data practices change in the future, this page will be updated to reflect that.
